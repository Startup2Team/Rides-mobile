FARE NEGOTIATION SYSTEM

After driver accepts:
Customer enters proposed fare.

Negotiation rules:

* Customer initial offer
* Driver counter offer
* Customer revised offer
* Driver revised offer
* Optional masked call simulation
* One final fare proposal

Final receiving party can:

* Accept
* Decline

No further negotiation allowed after final proposal.

If negotiation fails:

* Ride cancelled
* Driver returned to queue
* Next nearest driver searched

Realtime negotiation chat must use websockets.
